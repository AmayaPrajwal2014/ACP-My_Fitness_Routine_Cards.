My Fitness Routine Cards

You don't need hours in the gym to stay healthy. Here are a few simple, everyday habits to keep your body active and energized.

Morning mobility:

Spend 5 to 10 minutes right after waking up doing simple joint rotations and stretches to reduce stiffness and improve circulation.

Time: 10 minutes

30-minute exercise window:

Dedicate 30 minutes daily to intentional exercise, alternating between bodyweight strength (squats, push-ups) and cardio (walking, cycling).

Time: 20 minutes

Movement stretches:

Break up prolonged sitting by standing, stretching, or doing a few squats for two minutes every hour.

Time: 2 minutes every hour

Ambient step target:

Aim for 7,000 to 10,000 steps throughout the day by pacing during calls or taking brief post-meal walks.

Time: 15 minutes

Core and posture care:

Counteract desk slouching with daily chest stretches, glute bridges, and hip mobility exercises to protect your lower back.

Time: 25 minutes

Consistent hydration:

Drink a full glass of water upon waking up and keep a bottle nearby to sip continuously throughout the day.

Time: All day

Evening wind-down:

Finish heavy workouts at least 2 to 3 hours before bed so your body can cool down and prepare for deep, restorative sleep.

Time: At night

Fitness Tip: A good routine includes exercise, rest, healthy food, and consistency.
